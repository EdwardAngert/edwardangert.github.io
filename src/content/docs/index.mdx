## At work

### Transactive memory, you know?

When your go-to person wins the lottery tonight and never shows up to work again, who knows how to get the copy machine fixed?

What platforms do new hires need access to?\
Has anyone confirmed that these steps still work?

I make documentation that you can find and follow.

Everyone is a contributor, and everyone is a stakeholder.

Done well, documentation makes everything about the process and the product feel better and smoother.

</Column>
<Column>

### Documentation, broadly construed

Documentation isn't just that stuff you throw out when you get something new.

It's tire pressure numbers inside the driver's side door.\
It's the list of dishwasher-safe parts on the side of my humidifier's tank.

That's exactly where they belong.

- Button text and interface labels.
- Scope-of-support and MSA-type documents.
- The email that tells a new hire when to start, where to sit, and what the bathroom code is.
- FAQs, offboarding checklists, and the masking tape and Sharpie on a cabinet shelf that says which containers go there.

All documentation.

</Column>
</Columns>
